## Vercel
The free Hobby plan is for personal, non commercial use only (Vercel Terms, section 4: https://vercel.com/legal/terms). A business site with a lead form needs Pro.

## Texting (Twilio)
- Any business texting in the US has to register first. There is no exception for texting only the owners.
- Recommended route: a toll-free number with toll-free verification. No registration fee found, about $2.15 a month, Twilio says it reviews within three business days. https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/toll-free/console-onboarding
- Other route: a local number (10DLC). About $19 one time plus $2.65 to $3.15 a month, and up to a week or more to approve. https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/a2p-10dlc
- Twilio will ask for: legal business name, business type, EIN, address, a contact, the website address, a sample message, monthly volume, and how Lou and Tim agreed to get the texts.
- Could not verify: that toll-free verification is free (the docs list no fee), and the exact 10DLC brand fee ($4 or $4.50).
- Until it is approved the site runs in test mode: leads are saved and show on /admin, nothing is texted.
